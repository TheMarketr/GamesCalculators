## Start with one clear target

Enter your current level and the level you want to reach in the [Fortnite XP Calculator](/fortnite/xp-calculator/). The result shows the estimated XP remaining and turns that total into a rough number of matches from your own average XP.

## Use your average, not your best match

A personal best makes an optimistic plan. Use an average from several ordinary sessions, including quieter matches, so the estimate better reflects how you normally play. If your XP varies widely, calculate both a lower and upper scenario.

## Break the goal into sessions

Divide the remaining XP by the number of sessions available. A smaller session target is easier to review and adjust than one large season total. Recalculate after meaningful level gains rather than trying to follow an outdated plan.

## Leave room for changing XP sources

Quests, events and mode rewards can change. Treat the calculator as a planning estimate based on the inputs you provide, not as a guarantee of a particular reward rate. Follow the current in-game descriptions for the source you are using.

## Balance progress and performance

If frame rate is affecting play, use the [Fortnite FPS Calculator](/fortnite/fps-calculator/) to compare realistic hardware and settings ranges. Its output is deliberately a range because maps, updates, temperatures and background applications can all change actual performance.
