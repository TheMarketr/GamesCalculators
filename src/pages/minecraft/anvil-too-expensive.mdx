## What “Too Expensive” means

In Survival mode, an anvil operation is blocked when its required cost reaches 40 levels or more. Renaming alone can still be allowed in some edge cases, and Creative mode does not use the same restriction.

## Why the cost keeps rising

Every time an item is worked in an anvil, its prior-work penalty grows. The penalty sequence is based on powers of two: 0, 1, 3, 7, 15, 31 and so on. Combining heavily worked items can therefore push an otherwise normal enchantment past the limit.

## A practical combining order

1. Start with clean books and a clean target item where possible.
2. Combine books into balanced pairs before applying them to the item.
3. Put the item you want to keep in the left slot.
4. Check both book orders; one can cost less than the other.
5. Avoid repeated renaming or unnecessary anvil steps.

The goal is fewer total anvil operations on the final item, not simply the lowest cost for the first step.

## When to start again

If an item already has a large prior-work penalty, a fresh item plus newly combined books may cost fewer total levels. Compare the value of the item, its existing enchantments and the time required to replace it before deciding.
