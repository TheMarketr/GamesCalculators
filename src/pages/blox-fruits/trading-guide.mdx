## Value is only half the trade

A value total is a useful starting point, but demand affects how easily an item can be traded again. A slightly lower total with stronger demand may be more useful than a larger offer filled with difficult items.

## Build a cleaner offer

1. Start with the item the other player actually wants.
2. Add one or two useful items instead of filling every slot automatically.
3. Compare both total value and average demand.
4. Leave room for market movement when an update is close.
5. Confirm every item in the in-game trade window before accepting.

Use the [Blox Fruits Trade Calculator](/blox-fruits/trade-calculator/) to compare both sides and copy a shareable offer link.

## Reading the fair range

The calculator treats offers within seven percent as broadly fair. That range is a planning guide, not a guarantee: player preference, supply and recent balance changes can justify a different result.
