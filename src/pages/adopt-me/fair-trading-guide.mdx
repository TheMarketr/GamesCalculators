## Match the exact pet variant

Regular, Neon and Mega Neon versions should not be treated as the same item. Fly and Ride attributes can also change a pet's reference value. Select the exact version in the [Adopt Me Pet Calculator](/adopt-me/pet-calculator/) before comparing it with another offer.

## Compare complete offers

Add every pet on both sides. The [Adopt Me WFL Calculator](/adopt-me/wfl-calculator/) compares the totals and shows the difference as Win, Fair or Lose. A fair range is more useful than requiring two offers to match perfectly, because reference values and player preferences are not exact market prices.

## Look beyond the total

Demand affects how easily a pet may trade again. Age, availability and current player interest can also matter. If two offers have similar totals, consider whether you actually want the incoming pets and whether the offer improves your collection.

## Verify before accepting

Review the final in-game trade window carefully. Confirm every pet, variant and attribute after any change to the offer. Do not use a screenshot or copied result as a substitute for checking the live trade.

## Keep values in context

The [Adopt Me Value Calculator](/adopt-me/value-calculator/) uses editable reference data and displays its update date. Game updates and trading activity can change relative values, so use the result as a planning aid rather than a promise that another player will accept the trade.
